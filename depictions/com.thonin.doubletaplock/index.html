DoubleTapLock

Double-tap to lock — and to unlock.

Double-tap empty space to lock or unlock your iPhone.

  • Home Screen and app switcher: double-tap empty space to lock
  • Lock Screen: double-tap empty space to unlock — exactly like swiping up, so Face ID or your passcode is still required
  • Taps on icons, widgets, notifications, the flashlight/camera buttons and the passcode keypad keep working normally
