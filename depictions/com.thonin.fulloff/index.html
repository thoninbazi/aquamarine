FullOff

Control Center's Wi-Fi and Bluetooth really turn off.

Since iOS 11, the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth buttons in Control Center only disconnect you "until tomorrow" — the radios stay on. FullOff makes them switch the radio fully off, exactly like the switch in Settings.

  • Works everywhere Control Center shows them: the Connectivity group, the separate Wi-Fi and Bluetooth buttons, and the expanded network list
  • Turning them back on works as usual and reconnects to your network or devices
  • Fully off means AirDrop, Personal Hotspot and an Apple Watch's Bluetooth link go off too — same as Settings
  • No settings, nothing running in the background: it only acts when you tap
