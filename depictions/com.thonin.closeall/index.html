CloseAll

Close every app at once — keep the ones you lock.

Close all your apps from the app switcher in one go.

  • Tap Close All at the bottom of the app switcher, or swipe down anywhere in it
  • Hold a card for half a second to lock that app — locked apps show a padlock and are never closed by Close All (hold again to unlock)
  • The app playing music or audio is kept too
  • Apps really quit, exactly like swiping each one up
  • Works with any number of open apps; when nothing's left, you're back on the Home Screen
  • The button and padlocks only appear in the app switcher, never over your apps
