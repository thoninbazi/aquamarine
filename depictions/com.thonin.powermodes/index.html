Power Modes

Battery Saver, Balanced and Performance — plus a 3-day trial of every Pro mode.

Power modes that change real iOS settings — and always put yours back.

  • Battery Saver: Low Power Mode, dark appearance, Reduce Transparency & Motion, no auto-play, Background App Refresh off
  • Balanced: everything Power Modes changed goes back to your own settings (anything you changed by hand is left alone)
  • Performance: Low Power Mode off, background refresh paused, heat guard if the phone gets very hot
  • Live status: battery, Low Power Mode and the exact battery temperature
  • Uninstalling puts your settings back too

Free 3-day trial of everything in Power Modes Pro: Auto (switches by game, charger and your battery levels), Fast Charge + switch on plug-in, Gaming with per-game Game Mode rules, Ultra Saver and your own Auto levels. After the trial the free modes keep working.
