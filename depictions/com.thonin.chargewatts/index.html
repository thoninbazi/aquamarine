ChargeWatts

Live charging watts under your battery icon.

Shows how many watts are really going into your iPhone while it charges — a small pill right under the battery icon, on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and in portrait apps.

  • Reads the power chip's own charge counters, so it updates twice a second instead of every 20 seconds
  • Accurate to within a few percent of the battery driver's own energy counter
  • Hides when unplugged, in landscape apps and while the screen is off (no battery cost)
  • Touches pass straight through it
